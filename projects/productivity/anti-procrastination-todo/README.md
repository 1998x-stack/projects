# Beyond the To-Do List｜TODO List × 反拖延

**Status:** Published desk research · **Research date:** 2026-11-08 · **Category:** Human Productivity · **Sources:** 26 (16 Hacker News + 10 Product Hunt)

> **Product question:** Why do users keep organizing to-do lists yet struggle to start important tasks — and which interface patterns turn intention into action?

This research examines **8 representative products** (Todoist, TickTick, Sunsama, Llama Life, Amazing Marvin, Blitzit, goblin.tools, Flow Club), with additional signals from **NowDo, 3 & Done and Mom Clock**. The source material includes original Hacker News discussions and Product Hunt reviews/launch pages; user feedback is distinguished from company claims and independent hypotheses.

## Findings / 核心发现

1. **Recording ≠ starting.** Most task lists optimize capture, labels and planning; procrastination often appears at the first-action moment.
2. **Five useful mechanisms:** single-task focus, bounded daily capacity, small editable next steps, timeboxing and optional social accountability.
3. **No universal intervention:** some users want repeated prompts, others find reminders or countdowns stressful. Control and recovery matter.
4. **Trust is non-negotiable:** complaints about lost or duplicated tasks highlight that reliability comes before motivational design.
5. **AI is an aid, not an authority:** prioritization and task breakdown should be editable, explainable and reversible.

## Opportunity hypothesis: Start-First

**Proposed promise:** *Help someone begin today's most important difficult task, without replacing their existing task manager.*

**MVP flow:** Capture/import → choose one task → identify the blocker → edit a 2–5 minute first action → start a short session → recover gently if interrupted.

**Validation approach:** instrument difficult-task start rate and time-to-first-action; compare against a simple task-list baseline, interview early users and track voluntary repeated use. This is a research hypothesis **not a validated product, medical intervention or demonstrated PMF**.

## Research integrity

This is a purposive sample of public community discussions, **not** an exhaustive census, product hands-on test or causal experiment. Ratings are snapshots. First-person comments are not representative of all users; no adoption, retention, medical outcomes or willingness-to-pay claims are made.
